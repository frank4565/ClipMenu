ClipMenu 0.5.6

This patch keeps history sequence numbers continuous across range submenus. It supports Intel and Apple silicon Macs running macOS 12 or later. ClipMenu 0.5.0–0.5.5 users can update through Sparkle normally.

What changed

  • History items inside range submenus now keep their global sequence numbers instead of restarting from 1. (or 0.) in each folder. For example, with 10 inline items, the 11 - 20 submenu displays 11. through 20., and 0-based numbering displays 10. through 19. inside 10 - 19.
  • Command–1 through Command–0 shortcuts remain assigned only to the first ten history entries.

Validation

The Debug build and all 112 unit tests passed, including continuous subfolder numbering checks for 1-based, 0-based, and unnumbered history menus.
